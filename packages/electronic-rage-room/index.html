    Need Rooms Where You Can Break Things?

    Electronics Package Details – What’s Included

    We have all been there- mad at your computer, printer or other electronic items. You can’t break them at the office but you can here!

    • This package includes a set up with various electronics such as monitors, printers, keyboard or other various items.
    • Still need more? It will also include a small amount of glass items too.
    • We will include all safety gear & tools/weapons for your event.

    All sessions are appointment only. We do not take walk ins!

    Extra Items

    • We offer optional additional items to be added on to your booking during checkout.
    • You are allowed to bring additional items with you. However, we limit it to 10 glass items for no additional cost.
    • If you bring electronics with you, we do charge a $5 fee per item. All outside items brought must be clean and will be checked by staff before allowed to be smashed.

    Please Read Below for Important Item Details!

      • You must wear tennis shoes or work boots.
      • Absolutely no sandals, flip flops, ladies’ flats, hey-dude shoes, crocks or visible heels of any size.
      • Anyone with the wrong shoes will either not be able to participate or can rent shoes from us if available.

    • Our sessions have set start times and set end times.

      • To ensure you receive the full session time we need you to arrive 15 minutes early.
      • The 15 minutes prior to your session start time are used to check you in, go over safety gear and also safety of the room.
      • When booking your appointment please consider your arrival time as well and make sure that time still works for you.
      • For example for a 3:00pm session we need you to arrive at 2:45pm.
      • Absolutely no one under the age of 13 is allowed within the premises of the business, sorry!
      • Participants between Ages 13-17 MUST have an adult who will stay on the premises during the session.
      • ID is required for adults at check in to match against waivers. Minors will verbally provide their information.
      • Regardless of if you have 2, 3 or 4 people only 2 people are allowed in the room at a time. However the rooms all have windows and you will still be active with your group

    Once your session is booked you will receive a copy of your reservation in your email. Please look for that email and sign your waivers. We do all waivers electronically.
